Academic Homepage

Shir Lissak

PhD Candidate in Data Science, Technion

I work at the intersection of natural language processing and mental health, with a focus on suicide risk detection, emotionally supportive language technologies, and socially grounded AI methods.

My doctoral research at the Technion is supervised by Prof. Roi Reichart and centers on building data-driven methods that help identify risk factors, understand vulnerable populations, and improve the quality of support systems.

Research

Current Focus

Mental Health NLP

Developing methods for predicting suicide risk, surfacing overlooked risk factors, and analyzing language signals across online and clinical contexts.

Supportive Language Systems

Evaluating and improving large language models as emotional supporters, especially for vulnerable and underserved populations.

Social Impact AI

Building data-centric, socially grounded NLP systems that can support research, intervention, and responsible deployment in high-stakes settings.
